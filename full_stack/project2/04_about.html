Hotel Booking FAQs

Q

What are the advantages of online hotel booking?

A

Online hotel booking comes with great advantages for travellers.

  1. You can book hotels in India or International destinations in just a few clicks.
  2. Compare hotel prices, ratings, reviews and amenities before booking.
  3. Enjoy exciting discounts, cashback and seasonal offers.
  4. Book last-minute hotels at affordable prices.
  5. Choose secure online payment methods like UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card and Net Banking.
  6. Select from thousands of verified hotels across the world.
  7. Read hotel policies and cancellation rules before booking.
Q

In the current situation, where can I find safe and sanitized hotels?

A

Hotels with hygiene and sanitation badges follow strict cleaning protocols, daily room sanitization, contactless check-in, temperature checks, trained staff, fresh linens and regular disinfection of common areas. You can safely choose these hotels for a comfortable and secure stay.
